# MRI Finder AU — Alpha

A lightweight public map of Australian Government-published Medicare-eligible MRI practice locations.

- 421 official practice locations with LSPN, address and published coordinates
- exact Leaflet map pins and matching list
- simple state, place and billing-information filters
- no accounts, submissions, analytics or booking

Medicare eligibility does not mean a scan will automatically be bulk billed. Users should confirm the exact MRI, referral requirements, fee and availability with the clinic.

Location data attribution: Australian Government Department of Health, Disability and Ageing / Services Australia, [MRI Unit Locations](https://www.health.gov.au/topics/diagnostic-imaging/MRI-and-PET-locations), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
