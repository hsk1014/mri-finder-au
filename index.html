What this Alpha is — and isn’t

Location source
LSPN, practice name, address and coordinates come from the Australian Government’s public MRI Unit Locations layer.

Billing colours
These are limited published signals, not a price quote or personal Medicare eligibility decision. Purple simply means we have not added reliable billing information yet.

Before travelling
Call to confirm the exact scan, referral requirements, preparation, fee, appointment availability and latest arrival time.
