What this Alpha is — and isn’t

Bulk bill MRI units
Government-listed MRI locations are shown as Bulk bill units. This is conditional and does not promise that every patient or MRI will be bulk billed.

Private billing MRI units
Provider-listed MRI locations not matched to the government list are shown as Private billing for this Alpha.

Location accuracy
Private billing addresses and pins were checked against provider pages and/or Australian Government national address data. Confirm the address before travelling.

Before travelling
Call to confirm the exact scan, referral requirements, preparation, fee, appointment availability and latest arrival time.

Data: Australian Government Department of Health, Disability and Ageing / Services Australia under CC BY 4.0, provider-published MRI directories, and Geoscience Australia National Address Points (G-NAF) for address verification.
