What this Alpha is — and isn’t

Location sources
421 eligible locations come from the Australian Government layer. 22 no-licence locations come from I-MED’s current public MRI licence list and official clinic pages.

Coverage limit
There is no complete public national register of Medicare-ineligible MRI equipment. Other providers’ unlicensed locations may still be missing.

Billing colours
These are published signals, not a price quote. A no-licence MRI generally cannot attract a Medicare rebate, but the clinic must confirm the exact fee and funding pathway.

Before travelling
Call to confirm the exact scan, referral requirements, preparation, fee, appointment availability and latest arrival time.
